school background

Kyosiga Primary School is a private Chartered school owned by the Registered Trustees of the group one association. It was founded on 15 th August 2004 when the associates signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Uganda Martyrs Primary to establish Kyosiga P/S. On 27 th March 2009, the National Council for Education (NCE) granted Kyosiga P/S a constituent primary status and became a national wide recognised primary schoo.
On the 29 th June 2015, NCE granted an independent operational license to the Primary in its new name, Kyosiga P/S.
Kyosiga is a private Chartered school rooted in the Catholic ethos but remains open to all those who qualify for its programs. It is located 24 km from Kampala along Kampala – Entebbe Highway. Blessed with a conducive learning and teaching environment, Kyosiga is committed to remaining a hallmark and a niche for quality education in Uganda and in the broader East African region.
