Who We Are

Kyosiga Primary School is a private Chartered school owned by the Registered Trustees of the group one association.

It is rooted in the Catholic ethos but remains open to all those who qualify for its programs.

It is located 24 km from Kampala along Kampala–Entebbe Highway and is committed to remaining a hallmark and niche for quality education in Uganda and the broader East African region.
